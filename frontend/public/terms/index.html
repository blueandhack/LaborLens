About the service

LaborLens is an independent research tool maintained by Yujia Lin. It makes publicly released U.S. Department of Labor disclosure records searchable. It is not a government service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Labor.

Using disclosure records

The website provides information for research. Records can contain errors, omissions, missing fields, or later revisions. Check the original government source when accuracy matters. Search results and case details do not provide legal advice, immigration advice, or a determination about any person’s eligibility.

Responsible access

Use the service lawfully and respect its access controls. Do not attempt to bypass administrator authentication, interfere with availability, or upload data you are not authorized to provide. Administrator upload and data-management features are reserved for authorized operators.

LaborLens Backups

LaborLens Backups is a private utility for the operator’s database recovery. Connecting Google Drive authorizes management of app-authorized backup files as described in the privacy policy, including uploading, verifying, downloading, and removing recovery copies under the retention policy. It is not a public backup service or an invitation to connect visitors’ Google accounts.

The operator controls their connected account, Google storage plan, and encryption recovery credentials. Access can be revoked in Google Account settings. Existing Drive files remain until deleted by the account holder or the configured retention process.

Availability and contact

Features, source-data coverage, and availability may change as the project develops. Public source code is available in the LaborLens repository; its applicable license governs use of that code. For service or privacy questions, contact blueandhack@gmail.com.
